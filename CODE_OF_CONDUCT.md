# Code of Conduct

Orion Studio is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone.

Contributors must communicate constructively, respect differing viewpoints,
accept feedback in good faith, and focus disagreements on the work. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not
acceptable in repository spaces or project events.

Report violations privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub's
reporting tools. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take
other proportionate action. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical, with attention to the safety of everyone involved.
