# Orion Studio Preview Privacy Notice

## Scope and Non-Applicability

This page is an applicability notice for the open-source Orion Studio Preview.
It is **not** a privacy policy issued by a declared Orion legal entity, and it
does not invent an Orion company, operator, postal address, data controller, or
set of privacy promises.

No Orion-operated account, subscription, payment system, collaboration backend,
AI proxy, extension marketplace, or other hosted online service is offered as
part of the macOS Apple Silicon Preview. Downloading the Preview through GitHub,
using macOS services, or configuring a third-party integration creates a direct
relationship with those respective providers under their own terms and privacy
practices.

The application contains upstream and network-capable code. Users should review
their settings and the policies of any agent, model provider, source-control
host, extension source, or other integration they choose to enable. This notice
does not claim that every inherited or user-configured network feature is
available, operated by Orion Studio, or covered by an Orion privacy commitment.

## Upstream Zed Attribution

Orion Studio is derived from Zed and retains necessary attribution to Zed
Industries, Inc. The Zed privacy policy previously reproduced in this file
describes Zed's websites, software, accounts, subscriptions, and hosted
services. It is not the Orion Studio Preview privacy policy and does not become
applicable merely because this repository contains Zed-derived code.

Users who separately use a Zed product or service should consult the official
[Zed Privacy Policy](https://zed.dev/privacy-policy).

## Questions and Sensitive Reports

For a documentation question, open a
[GitHub Issue](https://github.com/orion-agents/orion-studio/issues/new/choose)
without including personal data or secrets. For a vulnerability or sensitive
security concern, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).
