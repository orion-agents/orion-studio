# Channels {#channels}

Channels are persistent collaboration rooms for shared projects, notes, and
voice sessions. They require an operator-deployed collaboration service; no
public channel tree is bundled with Orion Studio.

After the service is configured and you sign in:

1. Open the Collaboration Panel.
2. Use the `+` control beside **Channels** to create a channel.
3. Invite trusted members and review inherited subchannel permissions.
4. Join a channel, then use **Share** to expose the current project.
5. Use **Unshare** to disconnect collaborators from the project.

Channel notes and voice calls are stored or relayed by the configured operator.
Review that deployment's access, retention, encryption, and recording policies.
The `mute_on_join` setting controls whether your microphone starts muted.

Sharing does not copy each collaborator's local editor configuration. Guests
may have read-only access depending on server policy. Following another
collaborator affects only the active pane and stops when you move or edit.
