# Orion Studio Business Service Availability

Orion Studio does not currently assume that a public Business subscription,
organization dashboard, hosted-model plan, or consolidated billing service is
deployed.

Teams can use the open-source desktop application with local or self-hosted
infrastructure. An operator may offer managed organization features only after
deploying authentication, authorization, billing, privacy controls, audit
logging, support, and the relevant model or collaboration services.
