# Privacy for Operator-Managed Organizations

No Orion Studio organization service or privacy commitment is implied by the
desktop source alone. An operator that deploys hosted models, collaboration, or
organization management must publish its own privacy policy, subprocessors,
retention periods, regional data handling, and incident-response process.

Organization controls should default to no prompt, code, thread, or feedback
sharing. Server-side policy must be authoritative; hiding a desktop control is
not sufficient enforcement.

Provider API keys, gateways, local models, external agents, and terminal-based
agents remain subject to their respective operators and terms. See
[AI Privacy and Security](../ai/privacy-and-security.md).
