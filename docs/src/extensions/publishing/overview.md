# Publishing Extensions {#publishing-extensions}

> Orion Studio Preview and Dev builds do not provide a hosted extension registry. The workflow in this section publishes to Zed's upstream registry; it does not create an Orion-owned registry entry, and Zed's review and support processes are independent of Orion Studio.

To submit a compatible extension to the upstream registry:

1. Review the [publishing prerequisites](./prerequisites.md) and ensure your extension is ready.
2. Add an [accepted license](./license-requirements.md) to your extension.
3. Follow the [publishing guide](./publishing-guide.md) to submit it to the upstream Zed extensions repository.

After the upstream registry publishes your extension, see [Updating an Extension](./updating-and-maintenance.md) for how to ship updates, and the [FAQ](./faq.md) for its maintenance and staleness policies. To use an extension without an upstream registry submission, see [Developing Extensions](../developing-extensions.md) for Orion Studio's local extension workflow.
